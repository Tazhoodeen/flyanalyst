# Sources and attribution

Flyanalyst is a fork of Stonkfly, which is itself built on DOOMFLY. The chain
matters: almost none of the neural machinery here is ours.

- **This fork** adds only the retail layer: `sales_display.py` (weekly sales
  chart renderer), `retail_decoder.py` (retail labels over the same decoded
  neurons), `retail_run.py` (run loop and shuffled control) and
  `make_demo_data.py` (synthetic dataset). Everything else is upstream.
- The trading environment, execution guard, AgentKit bridge and the explicit
  two-compartment extension come from
  [Stonkfly](https://github.com/nftechie/stonkfly), MIT License preserved in
  `LICENSE`. They are retained unmodified; this fork does not use them.
- The connectome importer, inferred visual projection, spiking kernel and
  baseline-centered plasticity implementation are adapted from
  [DOOMFLY](https://github.com/nftechie/doomfly), copyright © 2026 nftechie and
  DOOMFLY contributors, under the MIT License. No Doom game assets, World Labs
  assets, movie clips or portfolio material are included.
- [MaleCNS v1.0](https://male-cns.janelia.org/) data: the MaleCNS
  collaboration and upstream contributors, distributed under the release's
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 terms. Downloaded separately; see
  `stonkfly/neural/datasets.json` and `sources.lock.json` for release URLs and
  checksums. Cite the dataset and its associated paper when publishing
  results. The retained graph and modeled physiology are derived
  interpretations, not an official dataset product.
- [Coinbase AgentKit](https://github.com/coinbase/agentkit) (Apache-2.0) and
  [Coinbase Advanced Python SDK](https://github.com/coinbase/coinbase-advanced-py)

- Scientific sources informing the model are linked in `docs/model.md`. No
  papers or figures are redistributed.
- `demo/demo_sales.duckdb` is generated by `make_demo_data.py` and is entirely
  synthetic. It contains no revenue, shop names or dates from any real
  business.
